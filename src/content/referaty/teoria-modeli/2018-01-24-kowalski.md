This is joint work with Marcin Chałupnik. I will talk about our paper (available on [https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.06960](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.06960)) which gives some basics about homological algebra of difference representations. We consider both the difference-discrete and the difference-rational case. We define the corresponding cohomology theories and show the existence of spectral sequences relating these cohomology theories with the standard ones. The main motivation of our work is to find a general difference counterpart of the generic cohomology of Cline, Parshall, Scott, and Van Der Kallen.
