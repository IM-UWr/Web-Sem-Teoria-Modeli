My talk focuses on ultraproduct constructions, mainly in the context of
groups equipped with (invariant) metrics. I will present some new
constructions of simple and amenable groups based on metric
ultraproducts and discuss connections with open problems. I will also
pay attention to concepts from continuous logic related to the talk
(especially the compactness theorem in the metric setting). This is a
joint work with Krzysztof Majcher and Martin Ziegler.
